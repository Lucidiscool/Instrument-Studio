The trumpet studio.

Model

Trumpet by Charlie Tinley, used under CC BY 4.0. GLB supplied by SceneView. Adapted lighting, scale, orientation and interactive valve overlays for this studio.

Sound

Trumpet samples from FluidR3 GM by Frank Wen, rendered by MIDI.js Soundfonts / gleitz, under CC BY 3.0. Samples are played at adjusted pitches, with a finite sustain length.

Listen & playing

Six short, built-in trumpet arrangements of public-domain melodies. These are sampled performances, not commercial recordings. No MIDI upload is needed.

Individual valve clicks play a representative note. The piano and song player illuminate conventional valve combinations by pitch class. This is an exploratory studio, not a complete trumpet fingering tutor.
